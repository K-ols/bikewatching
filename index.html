Understanding the Visualization

This interactive map displays bike-sharing activity across Boston and Cambridge. Green lines represent bike lanes, while circles represent individual Bluebikes stations.

Larger circles indicate stations with more bike traffic. Circle colors show whether stations experience more departures, more arrivals, or balanced traffic.

Use the time slider to explore traffic patterns throughout the day. Hover over a station to view its total trips, arrivals, and departures.
